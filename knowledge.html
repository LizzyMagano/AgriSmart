Botswana Climate

Botswana is semi-arid to arid. Rainfall is seasonal and varies by location and year. Drought is a major agricultural risk.

Rainfall + temperature + soil + crop type = key factors affecting agriculture.

Know Your Crops

Drought-tolerant crops

  • Sorghum – tolerates dry conditions
  • Millet – suitable under low moisture
  • Cowpeas – resilient in semi-arid areas

Water-demanding crops

  • Maize – requires adequate rainfall
  • Vegetables – higher water needs

Note: Crop tolerance depends on variety, soil, temperature, and management.

Know Your Soil

Sandy soils: Drain quickly, low water-holding capacity.

Clay soils: Hold water, risk of waterlogging.

Loamy soils: Balanced drainage and retention, favorable for many crops.

The same rainfall affects farms differently because soil characteristics vary.
