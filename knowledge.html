🌦 Botswana Climate Overview

Botswana has a semi-arid to arid climate. Rainfall is highly variable, ranging from less than 250 mm in the southwest (Kgalagadi, Ghanzi) to over 650 mm in the north (Chobe, Okavango). Summers are hot (30–35 °C), winters are cooler (15–21 °C), and droughts are frequent.

Major Crops in Botswana

  • Cereals: Maize, sorghum, millet.
  • Legumes: Cowpea, groundnut, mung bean, chickpea.
  • Root crops: Cassava, sweet potato, yam.
  • Horticulture: Vegetables (tomato, cabbage, onion, carrot, spinach).
  • Fruits: Watermelon, citrus, emerging orchards in Chobe.

Rainfall Classification

  • Low Rainfall: Less than 400 mm per year (Kgalagadi, Ghanzi).
  • Average Rainfall: 400–800 mm per year (Central, Southern, Kgatleng).
  • High Rainfall: More than 800 mm per year (Chobe, North East, Okavango).

Soil Identification Guide

  • Sandy Soil: Feels gritty, drains quickly, crops wilt within 4–5 days without rain.
  • Clay Soil: Sticky when wet, cracks when dry, retains water but poor aeration.
  • Loamy Soil: Balanced texture, holds water yet drains well, ideal for most crops.

Farmers can test soil by moistening and rolling it into a ball: sandy crumbles, clay stays sticky, loam holds shape but breaks easily.
