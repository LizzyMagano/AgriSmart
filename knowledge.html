🌦 Botswana Climate Overview

Botswana has a semi-arid to arid climate. Rainfall is highly variable, ranging from less than 250 mm in the southwest to over 650 mm in the north. Summers are hot while winters are cooler and droughts are frequent.

Major Crops in Botswana

  • Cereals: Maize, sorghum, millet.
  • Legumes: Cowpea, groundnut, mung bean, chickpea.
  • Root crops: Cassava, sweet potato, yam.
  • Horticulture: Tomato, cabbage, onion, carrot, spinach.
  • Fruits: Watermelon, citrus.

Rainfall Classification

  • Low Rainfall: Less than 400 mm per year.
  • Average Rainfall: 400–800 mm per year.
  • High Rainfall: More than 800 mm per year.

Soil Types by District

District Dominant Soil Characteristics Best Crops
Kgalagadi, Ghanzi Sandy soils Poor water retention, drought-prone Millet, sorghum, cowpea
Pandamatenga Vertisols High water retention Sorghum, rice
Southern Luvisols Fertile, well-drained Maize, vegetables
Okavango Delta Alluvial soils Moist, flood-affected Rice, vegetables

Soil Identification Guide

  • Sandy Soil: Feels gritty and drains quickly.
  • Clay Soil: Sticky when wet and cracks when dry.
  • Loamy Soil: Holds water well and drains properly.

Farmers can test soil by moistening and rolling it into a ball.
